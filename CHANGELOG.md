# Changelog

## [2.0.0] - 2026-11-18
The big update.
- **100 new levels** (110 in total) across 10 new worlds, with difficulty rising level by level.
  Levels 11-110 are procedurally generated and every one is verified solvable with the game's own simulation.
- **11 boss fights**, one after every 10 levels, each tougher than the last: health bars for you and the boss,
  screen shake, a second "enraged" phase, and attacks you dodge by shifting worlds (fans, rings, rain,
  slams with shockwaves, dashes, lasers and summoned orbs).
- **Tokens**: collect the coins hidden along every level; bosses pay a big reward.
- **Shop** in the main menu: 8 skins (with crowns, horns, halos and headbands) and 7 weapons for boss fights.
- **Choose your name** (Latin or Cyrillic letters). It appears in game, on the results screen and in the credits.
- **New look**: a different backdrop for every world, bloom/glow, film grain, soft shadows, deeper stone shading and haze.
- **New music**: a boss theme and a second level theme, plus 15 new sound effects.
- Level select is split into world pages; new FIRE control (J / F / C / Ctrl / mouse, gamepad B / right trigger).
- Saves from 1.0 still work: finishing level 10 unlocks the first boss.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-14
First public release (GitHub Game Off 2026).
- 10 levels, each with a hidden Memory Shard.
- Light/Dark dimension shifting with world-specific blocks, spikes, enemies and machines.
- Wardens, wisps, sentinels, levers & gates, crumbling bridges, moving platforms, lifts, checkpoints.
- Main menu, intro, level select, pause, settings, controls, credits and ending screens.
- Adaptive two-layer soundtrack, synthesised sound effects, shift shockwave and particle effects.
- Auto-save, best times, optional speedrun timer, keyboard/mouse/gamepad support.
- Windows, Linux and (unsigned) macOS builds.
